# Computer
![1 + 1 = 10](block:oc2:computer)

Computers provide a basis for building hardware capable of running an operating system or low-level software.

Read the [getting started guide](../systems/index.md) for a short explanation on how to get a working computer.

### Tiers
Computers come in four tiers. Tier 1 has fewer slots, and higher tiers accept larger [memory](../item/memory.md) and [hard drives](../item/hard_drive.md), and run faster. A tier 1 computer is cheap to build from copper, tier 2 is the regular computer, tier 3 needs gold and a diamond, and tier 4 takes netherite, a nether star and diamond blocks.

- Tier 1: 2 memory slots up to 4M, 2 hard drive slots up to 4M, 2 card slots, 1x clock speed.
- Tier 2: 4 memory slots up to 8M, 4 hard drive slots up to 8M, 4 card slots, 1x clock speed.
- Tier 3: 4 memory slots up to 32M, 4 hard drive slots up to 64M, 4 card slots, 1.5x clock speed.
- Tier 4: 4 memory slots up to 64M, 4 hard drive slots up to 256M, 4 card slots, 2x clock speed.

Sizes and clock speeds are the defaults; the server configuration can change them. Faster computers also draw proportionally more energy for their processor.

### Configuration
Configure the components in a computer in its inventory screen. To access this screen, either open the computer with a [wrench](../item/wrench.md), or switch to the inventory screen using the toggle button to the left of the terminal screen.

### Connectivity
To connect external devices to a computer, use [bus interfaces](bus_interface.md) and [bus cables](bus_cable.md). Note that computers must be explicitly connected to a bus with a bus interface.

### Energy
Usually, computers will require energy to run. Most modern energy generators producing RF energy should be compatible. Computers have a small built-in energy storage. However, this will usually not be sufficient to keep the machine running for any prolonged amount of time.
